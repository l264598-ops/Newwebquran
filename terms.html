Terms & Conditions

Effective date: 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this website, booking a demo class, or enrolling in any Quran or Islamic educational course with Teacher Bua Qasim Shigry, you acknowledge and agree to comply with the terms and conditions outlined below.

2. Trial Class Policy

All new students are entitled to one (1) complimentary 30-minute evaluation session with Teacher Bua Qasim Shigry. There is zero financial obligation associated with taking a demo class. Formal course registration only commences after mutual agreement on schedule and fees.

3. Class Attendance & Makeup Policy

Punctuality is essential for steady Quranic progress. If a student is unable to attend a scheduled live session, notice must be given via WhatsApp at least four (4) hours in advance so that a convenient makeup class can be scheduled within the active billing month.

4. Classroom Etiquette (Adab)

As an Islamic learning environment dedicated to the Word of Allah, both teacher and students are expected to uphold sincere respect, punctuality, and appropriate attire during video sessions. Distractions should be minimized in the student's learning space.

5. Monthly Billing & Cancellations

Tuition fees are billed monthly on a calendar basis according to agreed weekly frequency. We do not impose long-term contracts; students or parents may pause or discontinue classes at the end of any billing month upon simple notice.

6. Inquiries

For any clarifications regarding our educational policies, please reach out via our contact page or directly via WhatsApp.
