Biographical Overview

A Life Dedicated to the Words of Allah

Teacher Bua Qasim Shigry has dedicated over 16 continuous years to teaching the Holy Quran to learners of all backgrounds. Throughout this extensive teaching tenure, he has personally taught over 500 students across diverse age groups, from young children pronouncing their first Arabic letters to adults seeking fluency in recitation with strict Tajweed.

Rooted in authentic tradition, his instruction balances rigorous attention to precise articulation points (Makharij) and phonetics with genuine kindness, patience, and encouraging pedagogical manners.

Core Teaching Principles:

  • • Patience First: Mistakes are natural milestones of learning; correction is always delivered gently.

  • • Tailored Pacing: Lessons adjust to individual cognitive speed, ensuring zero stress for children.

Pedagogical Framework

Our Teaching Philosophy

Every child and adult deserves an uplifting, positive connection with the Holy Quran.

1. Phonetic Precision (Makharij)

Arabic possesses nuanced guttural and pharyngeal consonants. We train the vocal tract systematically from the earliest lessons, preventing ingrained recitation errors.

2. Habit & Consistency

Short, consistent sessions (30–35 minutes) 3 to 5 times weekly are proven far more effective than long, exhausting lessons, especially for young children.

3. Parent Collaboration

For young students, we maintain transparent open communication with parents, sharing weekly progress notes and recommended home revision routines.

4. Deep Spiritual Reverence

Learning the Quran is not merely an academic exercise; it is an act of worship. We instill love and reverence for Allah's holy speech in every student's heart.

5. Flexible Worldwide Timings

Living in North America, Europe, Australia, or the Middle East? We adapt to your family's timezone to ensure classes happen at a convenient, relaxed hour.

6. Lifelong Learning

It is never too late to learn or perfect Quran recitation. We have guided many mature adults who learned to read the Quran with confidence in their adult years.

Modern Virtual Classroom

How We Conduct Interactive Online Classes

All lessons are held 1-on-1 via high-definition video platforms (Zoom, Google Meet, or Skype). During each session:

  • ✓ Digital Mus'haf Screen-Sharing: The student and teacher look at the exact same high-resolution Quran page, with the teacher highlighting words in real time.
  • ✓ Active Audio Feedback: The teacher listens carefully to every vowel duration, Ghunnah timing, and Qalqalah bounce, correcting immediately.
  • ✓ Daily Sabaq & Revision: Each class begins with previous revision (Sabqi / Dhor) before introducing the new lesson (Sabaq).
