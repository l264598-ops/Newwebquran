Authentic Masnoon Supplications

Essential Daily Duas

Authentic prayers from the Sunnah for daily life with accurate Arabic vowels, transliteration, and translation.

Morning · Upon Waking Up

Dua Upon Waking

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

Alhamdulillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushoor.

"All praise belongs to Allah, Who brought us back to life after causing us to die, and unto Him is the resurrection."

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6312
Night · Before Sleeping

Dua Before Sleep

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

Bismika Allahumma amootu wa ahya.

"In Your Name, O Allah, I die and I live."

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 6324
Daily · Protection

Leaving the Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

Bismillahi tawakkaltu 'alallahi, la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

"In the Name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah; there is no power or might except with Allah."

Reference: Sunan Abu Dawud 5095 (Sahih)
Meals · Sunnah

Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَعَلَى بَرَكَةِ اللَّهِ

Bismillahi wa 'ala barakatillah.

"In the Name of Allah and with the blessings of Allah."

Reference: Al-Hakim / Abu Dawud
Study · Quranic Supplication

Seeking Increase in Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Rabbi zidni 'ilma.

"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."

Reference: Surah Ta-Ha (20:114)
Family · Quranic Supplication

Supplication for Parents

رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani sagheera.

"My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small."

Reference: Surah Al-Isra (17:24)

Selected Authentic Hadith

Verified traditions of the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) emphasizing Quran study, integrity, and mercy.

Virtue of Quran Learning

خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

"The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it."

Narrated by Uthman ibn Affan (may Allah be pleased with him).

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari (5027)
The Reward for Struggling Readers

الَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَهُوَ مَاهِرٌ بِهِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ، وَالَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَيَتَتَعْتَعُ فِيهِ وَهُوَ عَلَيْهِ شَاقٌّ لَهُ أَجْرَانِ

"The one who is proficient in the recitation of the Quran will be with the honorable and obedient scribes (angels), and he who recites the Quran and finds it difficult, stammers over it, will have a double reward."

Narrated by Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her).

Source: Sahih Muslim (798)
Purity of Intentions

إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى

"Actions are judged by intentions, and every person will get the reward according to what he has intended."

Narrated by Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him).

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari (1) & Sahih Muslim (1907)
Worship Foundations

Namaz & Quran Learning Advice

Practical guides compiled from Teacher Bua Qasim Shigry's 16 years of educational work.

The 5 Compulsory Prayers

Fajr (2 Sunnah, 2 Fard), Dhuhr (4 Sunnah, 4 Fard, 2 Sunnah, 2 Nafl), Asr (4 Fard), Maghrib (3 Fard, 2 Sunnah, 2 Nafl), Isha (4 Fard, 2 Sunnah, 2 Nafl, 3 Witr, 2 Nafl).

Prerequisites of Salah (Shuroot)

Ritual purification (Taharah via Wudu), clean clothes and place, covering the Awrah, facing Qiblah, entering the designated prayer time, and having a sincere intention (Niyyah).

Tajweed Rule: Noon Sakinah

Four rules apply whenever Noon Sakinah (نْ) or Tanween occurs: Izhar (clear pronunciation), Idgham (merging with/without Ghunnah), Iqlab (transforming into Meem), and Ikhfa (concealing with nasal sound).

Retaining Hifz (Dhor Routine)

Never memorize new Sabaq without thoroughly revising your recent lessons (Sabqi). Allocating 70% of study time to revision ensures permanent memorization.

Teaching Children With Gentleness

Praise effort rather than speed. A child who recites half a page with love and confidence develops a lifelong friendship with the Holy Book.
