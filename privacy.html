Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

At Online Quran Academy — Teacher Bua Qasim Shigry, we value the trust you place in us when enrolling yourself or your children in our online Quran learning programs. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, and protection of your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by students and parents when requesting demo classes or contacting us:

  • Student name, age group, and current Quran reading level.
  • Parent or guardian contact details for minor students.
  • Email address and WhatsApp phone number used for class scheduling and trial communication.
  • Timezone preferences and availability windows.

3. Use of Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to deliver personalized Quran instruction, arrange class meetings, coordinate schedules, and communicate academic progress. We never sell, rent, lease, or distribute student information to third-party commercial marketing entities under any circumstances.

4. Child Safety & Minor Privacy

We maintain stringent child safety standards in our virtual classrooms. For students under the age of 18, all communication, scheduling, and billing must be conducted through a verified parent or legal guardian. Parents are warmly welcome to observe live sessions at any time.

5. Local Browser Storage

Our website uses browser localStorage strictly to store non-sensitive frontend preferences, specifically your Digital Tasbeeh counter tally and dhikr selection. This information remains completely on your local device and is never uploaded to any server.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or wish to update or delete your submitted contact records, please contact Teacher Bua Qasim Shigry at info@quranacademyshigry.com or via WhatsApp.
