Payment Methods & Policies

How Fees & Payments Work

Transparent, secure payments arranged directly between the student and academy without intermediary markups.

No Upfront Payment Required

You only pay after you complete your free trial class and confirm that you or your child are completely satisfied with the teaching style.

International Payment Channels

We accept direct bank transfers, Wise (TransferWise), Remitly, Western Union, and PayPal, depending on your country of residence.

Monthly Post-Trial Invoicing

Fees are billed on a clean monthly basis at the beginning of each billing month. No long-term lock-in contracts; cancel anytime.

If a student must miss a scheduled class due to sickness or travel, simply notify the teacher 4 hours in advance to reschedule a makeup session.
